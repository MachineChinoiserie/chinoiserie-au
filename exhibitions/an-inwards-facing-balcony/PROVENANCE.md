# An Inwards Facing Balcony — provenance

## 22 August 2026 — catalogue families reconciled

- The surviving exhibition document `~ an inwards facing balcony ~` is treated as the primary catalogue source for work-family numbering.
- It records **Field Studies** as nos. **1–31** and **Diary Excerpts (This Is Not A Love Letter)** as nos. **39–65**, mixed media on paper.
- Surviving installation photographs independently show plinth numbers **43–44, 52–58 and 60**. These fall inside the Diary Excerpts run and therefore identify the photographed long-plinth sequence as **Diary Excerpts**, not Field Studies.
- A previous website state described Field Studies as the central-plinth works. That attribution was removed once the catalogue and photographs were reconciled.
- Field Studies titles remain public without forced drawing-to-title assignments because the forwarded working list and surviving numbering record are not being treated as sufficient proof of individual visual identity.
- Diary Excerpts number-to-title mappings are published because the contemporary exhibition catalogue supplies them directly. Number-to-drawing mappings remain unpublished unless a photograph or source scan proves the match.
- Evidence rule: correct the architecture when evidence improves; do not preserve a pleasing reconstruction after it becomes false.
