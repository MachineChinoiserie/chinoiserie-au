# Field Studies — provenance

Archive route / working title: **An Inwards Facing Balcony**

## 22 August 2026 — public-title correction

- Final exhibition poster evidence states **FIELD STUDIES**, **24 FEBTH–MAR17TH 2023**, **GALLERY CENTRAL**, **BY CHERISH MARRINGTON**. This is stronger evidence for the public exhibition title than the surviving working-document filename/title `~ an inwards facing balcony ~`.
- The surviving invitation independently gives **FEILD STUDIES BY CHERISH MARRINGTON** (spelling as printed) with the same Gallery Central dates. A contemporary Gallery Central/social listing also refers to **Field Studies**.
- The website therefore presents **Field Studies** as the 2023 exhibition title. The existing `/exhibitions/an-inwards-facing-balcony/` route is retained for archive continuity, and **An Inwards Facing Balcony** is preserved as a working/archive title rather than silently erased.
- The working catalogue also contains a same-name **FIELD STUDIES** body within the exhibition. Do not collapse exhibition-level naming and body-level naming merely because they match.

## 22 August 2026 — installed-state reconciliation

- **Field Studies** is supported as a work/body title by surviving wall-title photography and by the working catalogue. The website uses **Field Studies** for the middle installation as well as for the exhibition title where supported by final public material.
- The working catalogue describes the Field Studies body as **unsigned originals** plus **signed photocopies, each a 1/1 edition**. Individual title ↔ drawing mappings remain unresolved unless independently proven.
- **Diary Excerpts (This Is Not A Love Letter)** is a separate wall-hung group of **framed originals**, **mixed media on paper**, with **no reproductions available** in surviving exhibition paperwork.
- A surviving working catalogue gives Diary Excerpts a global numbering sequence of **39–65**. Other floor-sheet and installed labels appear to use a different local numbering sequence. Do not normalise those sequences until the mapping is proven.
- Separate wall-hung unframed originals are grouped under **CALL THE POLICE – I’M SCARED** in surviving sales material. One working catalogue gives the titles **My Flowers to You and Yours**, **An Anxiety Epic**, and **A Dreamy Wise Voice**, but numbering differs between working and installed materials; preserve the titles without forcing a definitive number map.
- Red dots visible beside wall labels are sale markers, not part of the works; preserve untouched archival photographs, but remove sale dots from public-facing cleaned images where practical.
- Miniatures were dispersed through the installation; at least one was placed in the room with the viewing machine. Exact placements remain to be mapped from photographs or the artist's annotated plan.
- Evidence rule: final public material + installed-state photographs + exhibition wall text outrank a convenient retrospective taxonomy. Working paperwork remains evidence, but conflicting numbering or naming must remain visibly unresolved until reconciled.
