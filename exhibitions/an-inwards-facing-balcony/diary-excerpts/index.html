Diary Excerpts

This Is Not A Love Letter / framed originals

record

Diary Excerpts (This Is Not A Love Letter) was a separate wall-hung group of framed originals. Surviving exhibition paperwork describes the works as mixed media on paper with no reproductions available, and installation photographs show the clustered framed hang with individual labels and sale dots.

numbering

A surviving working catalogue assigns Diary Excerpts a global sequence of 39–65. Other floor-sheet and installed labels appear to use a different local numbering system. The website does not normalise those sequences until the mapping is proven.
