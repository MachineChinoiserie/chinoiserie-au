record

Field Studies was used in the exhibition itself: surviving installation photography records the title on the gallery wall. A working catalogue also lists Field Studies as a distinct body of works, describing unsigned originals and signed photocopies, each photocopy treated as a 1/1 edition. The present website uses Field Studies for the middle installation while leaving individual title ↔ drawing mappings unresolved unless independently proven.
