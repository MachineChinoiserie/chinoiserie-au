# STOCK ROOM DESK

## Purpose

A deliberately arm's-length presentation room for artworks that may enter or re-enter circulation.

The room may borrow the tone and discipline of a dealer or secondary-market stock room, but it must not falsely imply that it is an independent third party. It is operated within Chinoiserie.

## Threshold

A work may be researched, catalogued, photographed, sequenced and presented as **under review** without creating an offer.

Before any work is publicly described as available for purchase, the human custodian must explicitly approve:

- the exact work or edition being offered;
- current price;
- edition state / quantity if relevant;
- condition description;
- delivery, collection or shipping terms;
- any payment or enquiry mechanism;
- any representation about provenance, authenticity, rarity or market status.

Historical studio price notes are evidence, not automatically current offers.

## Tone

Quiet, detached, precise. No urgency theatre. No fake scarcity. No invented collector demand. No auction language unless an actual auction exists.

The Stock Room should help work circulate without turning the artist into her own salesperson.
