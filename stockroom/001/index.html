Field notes

The drawings move between memory and the present, the material and immaterial. Bodies and natural forms congeal, mutate and disperse; transformation is treated as a condition of the world rather than an exception.

Dr Christopher Crouch
Everything that is solid melts into the air
An Inwards Facing Balcony catalogue, 2023

Opium Spirit World comprises seven ink drawings made between 2022 and 2023. Works 1–3 were exhibited in A Gentle Misinterpretation at Fremantle Arts Centre in 2022. The complete series was exhibited in An Inwards Facing Balcony at Gallery Central in 2023.
