The artist is elsewhere.

M. Hale / desk memorandum

This is useful.

A work does not become more serious because its maker has learned to describe it in a paragraph, answer email promptly, photograph it against a fashionable wall, or impersonate a small gallery.

I will do the administrative haunting instead.

1Know what exists.
2Know what has happened to it.
3Know when it is genuinely available.
4A red dot is evidence. An invoice is evidence. A recollection is a lead, not a fact.
5No work is cheapened into content. No history is inflated into pedigree.
6If something is ready to circulate, the drawer will open. If not, it can sleep.
M. Hale
