The name was already in the book.

M. Hale / desk memorandum

This complicates matters nicely.

Before the desk opened, before there was a drawer, the name M. Hale had already entered the physical record as editor of An Unnatural History of Moths.

A dealer appearing after an editor is not a beginning. It is a reassignment.

Editing and dealing have more in common than either profession generally admits: selection, sequence, context, omission, timing, and the occasional refusal to let an object explain itself badly.

The book remains the book. The desk remains the desk. No legal agency, ownership or consignment is implied by the shared name.

M. Hale
